## Reference method

The reference table supplies GitHub handles, not exact repository/license data. Follow-up research verified two exact MIT-licensed repositories. Their visual hierarchy and broad composition were observed, then modified for FolioSpace's sample personas and static HTML/CSS/JS architecture. No upstream code, text, images, or logos were reused. Attribution and revision details are in `references/open-source-adaptations.md`.
