GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Still wondering how it works? Here are a few things to know before getting started.

Do I need work experience to create a portfolio?+

Not at all. You can showcase class projects, student organizations, volunteering, research, coursework, and personal projects. A portfolio can grow with you.

Can I create a portfolio without a CV?+

Yes. We can help organize the information you already have. You do not need a finished CV to start.

Can I customize my portfolio design?+

Yes. We can discuss adjustments to colors, sections, content, and selected design details based on your needs.

How long does the process take?+

Timing depends on the selected design, the scope of the website, and how quickly the required materials are ready. We will confirm an estimated timeline before starting.

Will my website work on mobile devices?+

Yes. The portfolio layouts are designed to adapt to phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes. We can discuss using a personal domain. Domain and hosting costs, ownership, and renewal arrangements will be explained in your quotation.

How much does a portfolio website cost?+

Every project has different requirements. Contact us with your preferred design and needs, and we will provide a custom quotation.

Send its name and the changes you need. We’ll discuss the scope and quote.
