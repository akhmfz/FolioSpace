I’m a visual communication designer who likes ideas with a little tension — unexpected combinations, useful details, and visual systems that make people feel something. My practice moves between brand identity, digital design, and collaborative storytelling.

When I’m away from my desk, I’m usually visiting a small gallery, taking photographs around the city, or collecting packaging I can’t bring myself to throw away.
